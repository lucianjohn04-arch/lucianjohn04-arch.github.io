ProvenanceBot

The user agent you may have seen in your logs:

Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; ProvenanceBot/0.1; +https://lucianjohn04-arch.github.io/bot.html)

What it is

ProvenanceBot collects published reporting for a small, independent research project in the United Kingdom. The project keeps a record of what was reported, by whom and when, so that each statement can be traced back to its source, and uses that record to make and score forecasts about world events.

It does not republish your articles, sell them, or make them available to anyone else. It keeps the link to your page with everything it records, so that your reporting stays attributed to you.

What it reads

  • RSS and Atom feeds that sites publish.
  • Individual article pages linked from those feeds, one page at a time, with several seconds between requests to the same site.
  • Public Telegram channel previews (t.me/s/…), without joining any channel.
  • Public data services, through their published interfaces.
  • Your robots.txt and, where we have recorded one, your terms of use page, so that we know what you allow.

It does not log in, does not go past paywalls, logins or CAPTCHAs, does not use proxies to get around a block, and does not pretend to be a web browser. The text it collects may be analysed by automated tools, including AI language models, to work out what was reported.

The rules it follows

  • robots.txt. We read your robots.txt before collecting from your site. Article pages are fetched only where it allows both ProvenanceBot and *. If your robots.txt cannot be read because your server is failing, we fetch no article pages from your site until it can be.
  • Changes. For sites whose feeds we collect, we re-read robots.txt on every collection run and your terms page weekly. A change that touches automated access, AI use or storage stops collection from your site at once, until a person has read the change.
  • Your terms. Where we have read your terms and they bar automated access, we do not collect from your site.
  • Refusals. If your server refuses us (for example with a 403 or 429), we record that and stop. We never try again under a different name.
  • Pace. One request at a time per site, with a gap of seconds between requests.

How to opt out

Add this to your robots.txt to stop ProvenanceBot collecting anything from your site:

User-agent: ProvenanceBot
Disallow: /

Or block only part of the site, for example:

User-agent: ProvenanceBot
Disallow: /private/

This takes effect the next time we read your robots.txt, which we do before each collection run, several times a day. What we already hold is kept as a record of what was published, but nothing more is collected.
